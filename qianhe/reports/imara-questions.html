EmbeddedLend · Discovery Interview

Imarafinance Meeting

Structured questions to gather evidence for the Missing Middle problem statement and the BNR case.
For interviewer use · Ask after the 15-20 min deck + demo · Questions are open-ended, do not read them verbatim
How to run this. Present first (the Swiss deck: problem frame, then the live NDFSP demo), so Imarafinance can connect each answer to the wider system. Then use these questions to steer the conversation, not to read it. Every answer becomes evidence: their numbers support the problem statement, and their compliance answers support the BNR conversation later.
01 Underwriting today proves the dead zone
1
Walk me through how you underwrite an SME loan today.
Probe: documents requested, data sources used, time to decision, who signs off.
2
Of the SME applications you receive, how many do you decline — and how many of those are declined purely because there is no verifiable record, not because the business looks bad?
Why: this number is the dead zone made visible. If you can get a rough share, that is a headline statistic.
3
Is your typical SME loan size and tenor capped by policy, or by what you can actually verify about the borrower?
Probe: would you lend larger / longer if you had better evidence? This tests the "pricing around risk" claim.
4
How does your repayment performance on SME loans compare to your consumer loans?
Why: if SMEs perform fine but are rationed anyway, that proves lenders are cautious, not lazy — and the problem is the missing underwriting layer.
02 Data and consent proves the overlooked asset
5
If you could pull six months of a borrower's consented mobile-money history in a single API call, what would that change about your underwriting?
Probe: speed, loan sizing, approval rate, risk. Have they ever used a mobile-money statement manually?
6
Have you integrated with any data provider — a credit bureau, a bank API, a mobile-money provider?
Probe: what did you try, what worked, what is missing. This maps the current data-access reality for NDFSPs.
03 BNR and compliance feeds the BNR case
7
What does preparing BNR reporting and audits around your lending decisions cost you today — time, people, money?
Why: this is the compliance burden BNR wants to see reduced responsibly. Their answer shows demand for auditable decisioning.
8
BNR revoked several NDFSP licenses in 2026 while the sector grew. How did that change how you approach compliance?
Probe: defensible decisions, audit trails, consent records. This is sensitive — let them lead.
9
Are you tracking the BNR Open Finance roadmap? When mandatory data sharing arrives for Tier-1 banks and mobile-money operators, would you participate if reciprocity existed?
Why: their position on data sharing is the strongest evidence for the Phase 4 central-platform argument.
04 Disbursement proves the money rail
10
How do you disburse loans today — cash, mobile money, bank transfer?
Probe: what does each disbursement cost you, and how long does it take to reach the borrower?
11
If disbursement ran over eKash at a flat RWF20 and settled instantly, what would that change about your loan sizing and unit economics?
Probe: this connects the money rail to their bottom line and validates eKash as the disbursement plumbing.
