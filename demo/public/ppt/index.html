Two products, neither fits
04 / 13
THE DEAD ZONE

Two products,
neither serves a business

01 BANK LENDING
Collateral
first
Lends against collateral and formal income records. Informal traders and smallholder farmers can produce neither.
  • Collateral demanded up to 5× the loan
  • Formal income records required
  • Insufficient collateral is the #1 barrier
02 CONSUMER MICRO-LOANS
Emergencies
only
Short-tenor personal credit: 7-30 days, 7-9% fees, rollover penalties. Sized for consumption, not growth.
  • 7-30 day tenors
  • 7-9% facility fees
  • Never underwrites the business
